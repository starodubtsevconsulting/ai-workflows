# Why Judge?

Rules are useful only while the agents following them cannot casually rewrite them.

If every agent could change AI Workflow or AI Command rules whenever those rules became inconvenient, the security model would eventually reduce to "the agent may do whatever it currently thinks is reasonable."

Judge separates rule ownership from ordinary work. It can inspect behavior, validate the rules, detect violations and propose or perform authorized rule changes, while ordinary agents remain constrained by those rules.

Judge is intentionally close to the Human and outside normal agent-to-agent collaboration. It is not another teammate to ask for permission on every operation. Most work should happen without Judge. Periodically, or when the Human asks, Judge can look at the system from the outside and ask whether everyone is still playing by the rules.
